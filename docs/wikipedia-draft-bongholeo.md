'''Bongholeo''' is the stage persona of '''Mike Vintzileos''', an American [[medical cannabis]] advocate from [[New Jersey]] who is known for appearing at municipal government meetings dressed in a large purple [[bong]] costume and performing parody songs during public-comment periods.<ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="headynj-profile" />

Vintzileos is associated with Sativa Cross, a New Jersey cannabis-patient advocacy organization led by Edward "Lefty" Grimes.<ref name="headynj-profile" /><ref name="headynj-madison" /> Performing as Bongholeo since at least 2023, he has appeared at council and committee meetings in several New Jersey municipalities, including [[Madison, New Jersey|Madison]] in 2023 and [[Edison, New Jersey|Edison]] in 2024.<ref name="headynj-madison" /><ref name="nj1015-edison" /> A July 2026 appearance at a [[Cranford, New Jersey|Cranford]] Township Committee meeting was recorded and circulated widely online, drawing coverage from national and regional news outlets.<ref name="usatoday" /><ref name="stereogum" />

==Background==
Vintzileos is a medical-cannabis advocate affiliated with Sativa Cross, an organization led by Edward "Lefty" Grimes that has used theatrical tactics at local-government meetings to advocate for cannabis patients and expanded dispensary access.<ref name="headynj-profile" /><ref name="headynj-madison" /> As Bongholeo, he performs in a large purple bong costume and typically sings parody songs.<ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="headynj-profile" /> His social-media accounts, on which he maintains a [[YouTube]] channel under the name "Social Justice Waterpipe", identify the persona with Vintzileos.<ref name="instagram" /><ref name="youtube-channel" /> The persona's name has been styled in news coverage as ''Bongholeo'', ''Bong Holeo'', ''Bong-Holeo'' and ''Bonholio''.<ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="news12" /><ref name="nj1015-cranford" />

==Public-comment appearances==
===Madison (2023)===
In January 2023, Vintzileos appeared in the purple bong costume, carrying a guitar, at a Madison borough council meeting concerning a proposed ban on a medical-cannabis dispensary. There he performed a song titled "You can't get no weed here" and gave his name as Bongholeo, appearing alongside Grimes at the hearing.<ref name="headynj-madison" />

===Edison (2024)===
On October 9, 2024, Vintzileos appeared at an Edison council meeting dressed as a giant cannabis bong and cradling a baby doll in a matching costume, and was removed from the meeting after criticizing the council; local coverage associated the appearance with the act "Bongholeo and El Rampador".<ref name="nj1015-edison" /> Edison subsequently restricted the use of props at its meetings.<ref name="nj1015-edison" />

===Cranford (2026)===
On July 7, 2026, Vintzileos appeared at a Cranford Township Committee meeting wearing the purple bong costume and sunglasses and holding a baby doll dressed as a red bong that he introduced as his son, "Bongholito".<ref name="usatoday" /><ref name="news12" /> During the public-comment period he performed a roughly three-and-a-half-minute parody of [[Lizzo]]'s "[[About Damn Time]]", including the line "It's big bong o'clock / yeah, it's hit thirty", before leaving to applause and saying "love Cranford".<ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="usatoday" /> He was not arrested and left voluntarily after finishing his song.<ref name="stereogum" />

==2026 viral video and media coverage==
Video of the Cranford performance circulated widely online and was reported by ''[[USA Today]]'', [[Stereogum]] and [[TMZ]], with regional coverage from [[News 12 Networks|News 12 New Jersey]] and NJ 101.5.<ref name="usatoday" /><ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="tmz" /><ref name="news12" /><ref name="nj1015-cranford" /> Reporting noted that Vintzileos, the performer in the bong costume, was not the person arrested at the meeting; a separate attendee was arrested after separately taking the podium.<ref name="stereogum" /><ref name="news12" /> Any charges against that attendee had not been adjudicated at the time of reporting.<ref name="stereogum" />

==Reception==
News 12 New Jersey characterized the July 2026 meeting as having been disrupted by a "strange cast of characters".<ref name="news12" /> After the meeting, Cranford mayor Kathleen Miller Prunty publicly called for an end to the "antics".<ref name="news12" /> Writing for NJ 101.5, Eric Scott speculated that the persona's name was a reference to the animated series ''[[Beavis and Butt-Head]]''.<ref name="nj1015-cranford" />
